## Research hypotheses worth testing

1. **H1 — Change sensitivity:** Can a source-bound, versioned dependency graph conservatively select claims affected by a *real, authenticated* methodology revision? Benchmark against full recomputation; report false negatives, false positives and reviewer workload.
2. **H2 — Full trust boundary:** Can NaFT demonstrate that authenticated source identity, unit/QC semantics and upstream measurements are checked separately from cryptographic record lineage? A Sepolia hash is only a byte/provenance witness.
3. **H3 — Governance audit trail:** Can a tool-change event be linked to re-approval checks, qualified human sign-off and uncertainty records, without pretending an exploratory ICVCM recommendation is operative policy?
4. **H4 — Confidentiality:** Can a future privacy-preserving verification layer prove a bounded deterministic calculation without revealing restricted inputs? The uploaded ZKP paper is related work, not a NaFT implementation.

## Controls and provenance

- Five original **user-supplied** PDFs were inspected and locally SHA-256 hashed; see JSON for exact filenames, hashes, page anchors and scholarly source links.
- PDF SHA-256 values attest **these uploaded bytes only**. They are not publisher-authentication or paper-approval seals.
- IEEE Access and Caldarelli state CC BY 4.0 in their copies. The other two manuscripts' redistribution licenses were not verified; the ICVCM report displays © ICVCM 2026.
- **No PDF bytes or publisher figures are re-uploaded** in this public repository. Cite/link source records; verify licensing separately before further redistribution.
- No synthetic/real observations, climate-impact measurements, carbon credits, legal applicability, production claims or institutional authorizations are inferred.
- The ICVCM CIWP recommendations were expressly non-binding and non-consensus at publication (PDF p. 3); note its proposed 6.2 re-approval triggers (p. 23) and 6.10 pipeline/uncertainty documentation (p. 27).
- The source-status labels refer to the uploaded copies; an external check of current publication/retraction status and the official report's exact document URL remains outstanding.
